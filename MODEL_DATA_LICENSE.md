## Covered assets

- `New_Model_Ckpt/`: pretrained potential-energy and dipole model weights.
- `Initial_conds_TRPMD_300K/`: initial coordinates, velocities, and input template.
- `random_num_new.npy`: random-number table.
- `xyzbohr_3dscan_eckart.npy`: geometry grid.
- `mass.ttx`: atomic masses.
